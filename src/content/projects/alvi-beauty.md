<p>Alvi Beauty is a booking product for at-home beauty services. Romarianec Tech contracted me for the backend and the parts of the system architecture that had to hold the whole thing together.</p>

<p>The product spans four mobile apps (iOS and Android for customers and for providers), a web booking flow, a provider dashboard for schedules and bookings, and a super-admin dashboard with full visibility and control. The main risk was consistency: six surfaces sharing one set of users, bookings, and provider data, and one backend that had to keep them agreeing without becoming hard to change.</p>

<p>I built that backend on Node.js and MongoDB, with the mobile apps in React Native so both platforms ship from one codebase. The team was two developers, a designer, and a QA engineer.</p>
